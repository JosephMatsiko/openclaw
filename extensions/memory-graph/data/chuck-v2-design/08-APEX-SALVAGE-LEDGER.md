# Apex Salvage Ledger

**Status**: V3 implementation input, not independent doctrine.
**Date**: 2026-04-27
**Purpose**: preserve useful primitives from the Apex/OpenClaw "beautiful mess" without blindly carrying forward brittle drivers, unsafe names, stale assumptions, or pre-Kernel autonomy.
**Coverage**: initial high-signal pass plus a broader inventory pass across `Projects/openclaw`, `~/Documents`, `.openclaw`, LaunchAgents, and local OpenClaw skills. This is a living ledger; it should be re-run after major cleanup or migration.

The old Apex pile is not trash. It is a field log: many scripts encode hard-won lessons about Joseph's Mac, subscriptions, browser sessions, receipts, memory pressure, and fleet behavior. V3 should salvage those lessons deliberately.

## Global Inventory Snapshot

The 2026-04-27 global pass found:

- about 13,038 candidate markdown/script/config files under the broad search roots
- 99 Apex scripts in `extensions/memory-graph/scripts/`
- 107 capabilities already registered in `~/.openclaw/workspace/state/apex-capabilities.json`
- 14 additional scripts found by `apex-capability-derive.mjs scan`
- 17 derived capabilities now missing from current runtime scan
- 21 relevant LaunchAgent files for OpenClaw/Apex/Chuck-era jobs
- a rich `.openclaw/workspace/state/` layer with fleet, health, selector, frontier, ring, curator, docket, event, and capability state
- a local skill surface with Apex-specific skills plus third-party operational skills

## Salvage Rule

Every candidate is classified as one of:

- **Port now**: directly useful for Kernel/Fleet/Surface preflight with small hardening.
- **Port behind Kernel**: valuable, but only after authority-diff, receipts, resource gating, and approval policy exist.
- **Quarantine**: keep as reference, but do not execute until the protocol is proven by canary/eval.
- **Rename and narrow**: preserve the legitimate mechanism, replace old language or broad behavior with V3 policy boundaries.
- **Retire**: stale under current constraints or superseded by a safer primitive.

## Immediate Copy-Forward Invariants

1. Every non-trivial surface adapter needs a real canary, not just cookie or app presence.
2. Perplexity Mac is unhealthy until the incognito protocol can prove fresh-session safety before the prompt is submitted.
3. Same-family surfaces strengthen surface confidence but do not increase independent family count.
4. Source-of-truth readers beat memory. Fleet membership, LaunchAgents, capability inventory, and health should be derived from disk/runtime state.
5. Dissent registers are permanent artifacts. A resolved fracture must keep the losing/outlier claim for later calibration.
6. Scorers and executors should rotate by family. No model family grades its own high-stakes work twice.
7. Resource pressure is admission control, not a warning after the fact.
8. The old "dominance" instinct is only acceptable inside legitimate authority: owned data, subscribed surfaces, local files, public sources, and operator-provided documents.

## Quarantine List

These pieces contain useful mechanics but must not run as-is:

- `scripts/research-perplexity-mac.mjs`: no-submit incognito/fresh-thread lease proof has passed; a submit/readback OCR nonce proof has passed once; keep unattended Fleet prompt submission gated until repeatability passes from answered-thread and home-screen states.
- `scripts/apex-dominance.mjs`: until rewritten as lawful evidence retrieval with explicit source policy.
- `scripts/apex-exfil*.mjs`: until renamed and scoped to owned-account archive/data portability.
- `scripts/apex-better.mjs`: until autocreate/autopromote are blocked behind authority-diff and operator approval.
- `scripts/apex-computer.mjs`: until authority-diff scopes and protected-path checks wrap every action.
- `scripts/watchers/index.mjs`: until the watcher/job registry is converted into a reviewed daemon matrix.
- Any web/app driver that only proves cookies/session presence instead of successful submit/readback.
- Any LaunchAgent that performs mutation without a current owner, leash, receipt path, and kill switch.

## Retire Or Defer

- Ollama-specific voice execution is deferred because Ollama was intentionally uninstalled on 2026-04-27. Keep model-eval lessons, but do not count `ollama-local` healthy.
- Old panel scripts that dispatch full prompts to every surface without Scout/Deepen/Resolve should become compatibility shims, not the V3 default.
- Model-computed arithmetic scores such as `severity × specificity ÷ √vendor_count` should be retired unless the arithmetic is performed by deterministic code over explicit fields.
